# Privacy policy

This is a personal, single-user project. Its Google OAuth app is used only by the repo owner, to read the owner's own health data.

- **What it reads:** health data from the owner's Google account (heart rate, HRV, sleep, SpO2, respiratory rate, skin temperature, steps), through read-only scopes.
- **Where it goes:** the owner's private AWS account, encrypted, with no public access. It's never shared, sold or sent to anyone else.
- **Revoking access:** remove the app at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
